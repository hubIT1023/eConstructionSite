- **Store Manager**: Daily operations, discount approval, and restock dispatch.
- **POS Cashier**: Counter sales, barcode scanning, and order finalization.
- **Order Processing Staff**: Warehouse picking, packing, and shipment tracking.
- **Store Incharge**: Physical receiving and depot inventory auditing.
