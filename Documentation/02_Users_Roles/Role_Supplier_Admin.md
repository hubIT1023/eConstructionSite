```text
Status: Verified
```

- **Authentication Table**: `tbl_supplier_users` (role = 'Admin')
- **Session Key**: `$_SESSION['supplier']`
- **Access Route**: `/supplier/`
- **Key Capabilities**:
  - Manage store staff accounts and role assignments.
  - Create and edit store catalog products and construction specs.
  - Configure customer and category discount policies.
  - Access POS terminal and approve cashier discount overrides.
