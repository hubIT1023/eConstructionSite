## Customer Segments

1. **Retail / DIY Consumers**: Occasional purchasers buying tools and home improvement items; standard retail pricing and automated checkout.
2. **Trade Contractors / Commercial Accounts**: High-volume commercial builders eligible for agreed tier pricing, customer-specific discounts (`tbl_supplier_customer_discounts`), and fast POS trade counter pickup.
