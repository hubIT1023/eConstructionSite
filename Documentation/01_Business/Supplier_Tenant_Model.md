## Tenant Autonomy & Platform Oversight

1. **Independent Storefronts**: Each registered supplier has an independent catalog, branding details, and staff user hierarchy.
2. **Discounting Boundaries**: While suppliers can create customer-specific and category-specific discounts, they cannot exceed the SaaS `discount_ceiling_percentage` configured by the platform super administrator.
3. **Staff Roles**: Tenants configure their own staff rosters with tailored operational permissions (Cashier, Store Incharge, Manager, Admin).
