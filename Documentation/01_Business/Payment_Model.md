## Supported Payment Rails

1. **Stripe Elements**: Credit and debit card tokenization with direct bank capture.
2. **PayPal Express**: PayPal wallet integration with IPN notification hooks.
3. **Bank Transfer (Manual)**: Offline wire transfer with invoice reference verification.
4. **POS Cash / In-Store Terminal**: Direct cash tender recording with change calculation.
