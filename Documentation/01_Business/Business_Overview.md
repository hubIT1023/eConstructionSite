## 1. Executive Summary

**eConstruction Supply SaaS** is a dedicated multi-supplier B2B and B2C digital marketplace and store management ecosystem designed specifically for the building, civil engineering, hardware, and construction supply industries.

The platform provides a unified digital bridge between:
- Independent construction supply merchants, timber yards, and hardware stores (Suppliers).
- Professional building contractors, trade specialists, and DIY retail consumers (Customers).
- Platform SaaS Operators managing marketplace commissions and store standards (SaaS Admin).

---

## 2. Core Business Value Drivers

1. **Multi-Store Aggregation**:
   Customers can purchase heavy structural materials (cement, rebar, structural timber) and finishing supplies from multiple local yards in a single digital cart.
2. **Dedicated In-Yard Point of Sale (POS)**:
   Supplier staff can use the integrated POS terminal at the trade counter to process drive-through contractor orders, scan barcodes, and immediately adjust live inventory.
3. **Multi-Level Trade Pricing & Discount Policies**:
   Suppliers can offer custom discount agreements to trade accounts, volume tiers for bulk orders, and category-level sales, all bound by administrative discount ceilings.
4. **Inter-Branch Restock Workflow**:
   Suppliers operating multiple physical depots can request and transfer stock seamlessly to prevent site delays.
