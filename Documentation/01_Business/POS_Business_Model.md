## Trade Counter POS Operations

1. **Counter Sales Velocity**: Builder yards experience high transaction volume during morning trade hours (6:00 AM - 9:00 AM). The POS interface prioritizes rapid barcode scanning, live stock checks, and quick tender options (Cash, Credit Card, Trade Account).
2. **Supervisor Authorization**: Prevents cashier margin leakage by requiring supervisor PIN verification on large discount overrides.
3. **Real-Time Inventory Synchronization**: Instant stock deduction prevents double-selling inventory allocated to online orders.
