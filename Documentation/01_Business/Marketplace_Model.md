## Marketplace Economics & Commission Flow

The eConstruction Supply platform operates as a multi-vendor digital marketplace for construction supplies and heavy materials.

1. **Merchant Monetization**:
   Suppliers list inventory on the platform. The platform collects a configurable commission percentage (`tbl_supplier.commission_percentage`, e.g., 5.00% to 15.00%) on every completed digital transaction.
2. **Customer Pricing & Transparency**:
   Prices displayed on the storefront include VAT/GST based on regional store policies. Customers receive unified order invoices itemizing delivery and materials.
3. **Fulfillment Split**:
   When an order contains items from multiple stores, shipping is computed per supplier dispatch location.
