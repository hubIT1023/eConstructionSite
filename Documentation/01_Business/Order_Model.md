## Order Types

1. **Online Marketplace Orders**: Created by registered public customers through the storefront checkout, paid via Stripe, PayPal, or Bank wire transfer.
2. **In-Store POS Orders**: Generated at physical merchant counters by cashiers, paid via Cash or Card terminal.
3. **Restock Requisitions**: Inter-store inventory requests created between branches to rebalance depot stock levels.
