## 3-Level Category Taxonomy

1. **Top Category** (`tbl_top_category`): Broad domain (e.g. *Building Materials*, *Timber & Joinery*, *Plumbing & Drainage*).
2. **Mid Category** (`tbl_mid_category`): Sub-category (e.g. *Structural Timber*, *Cement & Aggregates*).
3. **End Category** (`tbl_end_category`): Specific product class (e.g. *Treated Pine Framing*, *Portland Cement 20kg*).
