## 1. Tenant Operational Dashboard

Each supplier merchant logs into their dedicated back-office portal at `/supplier/login.php` to access:
- **Daily Sales & Revenue Analytics**: Real-time sales charts and order volume counters.
- **Stock Depletion Warnings**: Low-inventory alert badges for fast-moving building supplies.
- **Pending Restock Requisitions**: Outbound/inbound inventory transfer requests across store branches.
- **Trade Discount Configuration**: Custom discounting policies bound by the store's approved ceiling.
